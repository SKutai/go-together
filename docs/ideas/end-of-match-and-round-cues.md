# Ending cues for a round and a match

Status: deferred 2026-09-19 by the project owner. Waiting on a decision about
the warning threshold, which is a rules question rather than a presentation
one. Nothing in this file describes current behaviour; see
[`README.md`](README.md).

## The idea

Signal that a timed round or a timed match is about to end, rather than having
it simply stop.

Three parts, in the order they were discussed:

1. A cue when the **match** is ending.
2. A matching cue when a **round** is ending. The owner's position is that the
   two belong together - a match-ending cue alone would be the only warning of
   its kind in the game, and the asymmetry would be strange.
3. Something **visual** alongside the audio. The suggestion on the table is a
   pulse emitted from the board's border. Nothing about the shape of that
   effect is settled.

## Why the visual half is not decoration

This came out of an accessibility review, and the visual is the part that makes
the audio safe to add.

An audio-only warning would be a new instance of exactly the pattern that
review was working to remove. When this was written the two clocks were not
symmetric: an active Round Time forced its panel visible and the preference
could not suppress it, while the Match clock's readout was a preference that
defaulted to off. Since 2026-09-24 the two behave alike - enabling either
timed rule switches its readout on, and the player may switch it off again
(`apply_game_toggle` in `src/ui/settings/mod.rs`, described in
[`../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../ARCHITECTURE.md#configuration-ownership)) - and the
round clock additionally has a bar below the board that no preference
removes. The Match clock has no such bar. That is what keeps the argument
here alive rather than settling it: a player who hid the Match timer, or who
never looked at the top of the screen, would still get an audio-only warning
that a match is about to end - unavailable to deaf and hard-of-hearing
players, to anyone playing with Game Sound Effects muted, and to anyone with
the device volume down. Pair the cue with a visible signal and that problem
does not arise.

The open item this used to track separately - whether enabling a Match Time
limit should reveal the match clock - landed with the change above, so that
part of the visual requirement is met; the warning itself is still unmet.

## What it would cost

**A Replay schema version.** `GameCue` (`src/cue.rs`) is a persisted
vocabulary: its `snake_case` variant names are part of the on-disk format from
schema v3 on, carried by `ReplayEventKind::MatchSound`. The current version is
v6. Adding a sound is an established move rather than a novel one - the enum is
`#[non_exhaustive]` precisely so consumers carry a wildcard arm, and
`OpeningDuplicate` arrived with v4 - but it is a version bump with the
migration discipline in [`../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../ARCHITECTURE.md#persistence-boundaries)
and [`../../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#changing-persisted-data)
attached to it.

**A rules decision, not a presentation one.** A warning threshold - "ten
seconds left", say - changes when a cue fires and therefore what a Replay
records. It belongs in the immutable `MatchSettings` snapshot each match takes,
alongside the existing bounded numeric rules, not in `GamePreferences` beside
the volume levels. Deciding it is what this idea is waiting on.

**Not the first animation in the game any more.** When this was written
Go Together had essentially no motion: the only two systems that read
`Time` in the UI were the Home-screen marimba Easter egg and Replay playback
advance, and the live board was painted as a pure function of cell state.
Since 2026-10-03 the capture indication in `src/ui/match_screen/capture.rs`
animates on the live board, and it arrived with the policy a border pulse
would have needed: `src/app/motion.rs` holds a Reduce Motion preference and
the browser's `prefers-reduced-motion` signal, and requires that whatever an
animation shows also has a static form. A pulse here would read that
resource the same way, and would need its own static form - which the
visible readouts above already are.

## Open questions

- What is the threshold, and is it player-configurable or fixed?
- Does the round-ending cue fire every round, or only when a Round Time limit
  is enabled? A round with no clock has no ending to warn about.
- Is the match-ending cue distinct from the round-ending cue, or the same sound
  in a different context? Distinct sounds mean two new `GameCue` variants.
- What does the pulse look like, and does it read at all for a player looking
  at their own side of the board rather than the border?
- Every sound the game ships - the marimba notes and the Round 0 clack alike -
  is generated by `tools/generate_marimba.py`, whose `--check` mode is one of
  the required checks and whose expected-file set is written into the script.
  A new sound has to come from that generator and be added to that set, not
  dropped into `assets/audio/` by hand.
